Flipora Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flipora handles information when you use the Flipora marketplace website or mobile app.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Flipora, we may process account information such as your email address and profile details; listing information such as item descriptions, prices, photos, videos, and seller location information you choose to provide; messages exchanged through marketplace features; and transaction-related information needed to operate purchases, memberships, advertising, and seller payouts.

Payments and seller payouts

Payment card information and seller payout onboarding are handled by Stripe. Flipora does not need to store your full payment-card number or bank-account credentials in its website database.

Service providers

Flipora uses service providers including Supabase for account and marketplace data services, Stripe for payments and seller payouts, and Netlify for website hosting and server functions. These providers process information as needed to provide their services.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, publish listings, provide buyer and seller communication, process payments and memberships, support seller payouts, prevent abuse, operate marketplace features, and provide customer support.

Location information

Flipora may display a seller location, city, ZIP code, or public meetup location when a seller chooses to enter one. Do not post a private home address unless you intend for it to be visible to other users.

Your choices

You may update profile information through your account. You may stop using Flipora at any time. If you need help with account or privacy questions, contact Flipora using the information below.

Children

Flipora is not intended for children under 13. Users who are not old enough to enter binding agreements in their location should use the service only with appropriate parent or guardian involvement.

Security

Flipora uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Flipora features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.
